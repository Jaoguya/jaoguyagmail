Inbox Trays

Your Gmail inbox sorted into Important, General and Ads. Runs entirely in your browser.

Sort your inbox in three trays

  • Important — security alerts, receipts, job applications and email from real people.
  • General — social and job-site notifications.
  • Ads — promotions and newsletters.
  • Cleaned up — everything this app moved to Trash, with a Recover button.

Your email never leaves your browser: the page talks to Gmail directly with a short-lived token that is kept only in memory.

Add your Google OAuth Client ID to config.js first — see docs/google-oauth-setup.md.
